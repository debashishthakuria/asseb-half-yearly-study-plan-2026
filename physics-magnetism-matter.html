Magnetism & Matter

Bar magnets, magnetic dipoles, Gauss’s law for B and magnetic materials.

NCERT Physics Part I · chapter 5 · PDF pages 177–207

Understand the idea

A bar magnet acts as a magnetic dipole. Magnetic field lines are closed loops: there are no isolated magnetic poles in this treatment. Materials respond differently to an applied field.

Derivation · Torque on a magnetic dipole

For a small current loop in uniform B, opposite sides experience equal and opposite forces. Their resultant force is zero but they form a couple. The torque is τ = IAB sinθ for one turn, or NIAB sinθ for N turns. Defining m = NIA gives τ = mB sinθ, tending to align m with B.

Worked example · Dipole torque

A loop has magnetic moment 0.20 A·m² in a 0.50 T field at 30° to B. Find its torque.

τ = mB sin30° = (0.20)(0.50)(0.5) = 0.05 N·m.
