Potential & Capacitance

Potential, equipotentials, dipole energy, capacitors, dielectrics and stored energy.

NCERT Physics Part I · chapter 2 · PDF pages 55–96

ConceptFormulasDerivationExampleQuestions

Understand the idea

Potential is electric potential energy per unit charge. Electric field points towards decreasing potential. Capacitance measures charge stored per volt; the dielectric changes capacitance and may change charge or voltage depending on what is held fixed.

Derivation · Parallel-plate capacitor

Take two large plates of area A, separation d and charges ±Q. Neglect fringing; between the plates the fields of the two sheets add.

E=σε0=Qε0AE=\frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}=\frac{Q}{\varepsilon_0 A}E=ε0​σ​=ε0​AQ​

The potential difference is field times separation. Substitute it into the definition of capacitance.

V=Ed=Qdε0AV=Ed=\frac{Qd}{\varepsilon_0 A}V=Ed=ε0​AQd​
C=QV=ε0AdC=\frac{Q}{V}=\frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}C=VQ​=dε0​A​

If a dielectric completely fills the gap, replace vacuum permittivity by its dielectric value.

C=κε0AdC=\frac{\kappa\varepsilon_0 A}{d}C=dκε0​A​

Worked example · Series capacitors

A 2 μF and a 3 μF capacitor are in series across 10 V. Find Q and the voltage across 2 μF.

C_eq = (2×3)/(2+3) = 1.2 μF; Q = C_eqV = 12 μC. V₂ = Q/C₂ = 6 V (and V₃ = 4 V).
