1. Field of an infinite line via Gauss’s law

Choose a closed coaxial cylinder of radius r and length L around an effectively infinite, uniformly charged line. The field is radial and constant on the curved side; end-cap flux is zero.

2. Parallel-plate capacitor

E=σε0=Qε0AE=\frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}=\frac{Q}{\varepsilon_0 A}E=ε0​σ​=ε0​AQ​

4. Field around a long straight wire

Choose a circular Amperian path of radius r around an effectively infinite, straight wire. By symmetry, the magnetic field is tangent to the circle and has constant magnitude.

∮B⋅dℓ=B(2πr)=μ0I\oint\mathbf{B}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}=B(2\pi r)=\mu_0 I∮B⋅dℓ=B(2πr)=μ0​I

Solve for B; use the right-hand grip rule for its direction.

B=μ0I2πrB=\frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}B=2πrμ0​I​

5. Torque on a magnetic dipole

6. Motional emf of a rod

A straight conducting rod of length ℓ moves perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. Charges feel a magnetic force until the electric force from separation balances it.
