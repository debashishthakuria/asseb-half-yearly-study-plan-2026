1. Electric Charges & Fields

Coulomb’s lawF = k|q₁q₂|/r²; k = 1/(4πε₀)Force along the line joining point charges.
Electric fieldE = F/q₀; E = k|q|/r²Field of a point charge; direction matters.
Flux and GaussΦ = ∫E·dA; ∮E·dA = Q_enclosed/ε₀For a uniform field on a plane: Φ = EA cosθ; θ is to the surface normal.
Dipolep = q(2a); τ = pE sinθp points from negative to positive charge.
Symmetry resultsE_line = λ/(2πε₀r); E_sheet = σ/(2ε₀)Infinite line and infinite non-conducting sheet.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 5–54. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

2. Potential & Capacitance

PotentialV = kq/r; ΔU = qΔVPotential is a scalar; take care with charge signs.
Field–potentialE = −dV/dr (along r)Use the negative gradient in the field direction.
CapacitanceC = Q/V; C_parallel plate = ε₀A/dWith fully filled dielectric: C = κ ε₀A/d.
NetworksC_parallel = ΣCᵢ; 1/C_series = Σ1/CᵢParallel: same V. Series: same charge magnitude.
EnergyU = ½CV² = Q²/(2C) = ½QVUse the correct constraints after disconnecting a battery.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 55–96. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

3. Current Electricity

Current and driftI = neAv_d; J = I/An: carrier density, e: charge magnitude, A: cross-section.
ResistanceV = IR; R = ρL/AFor a metal near a reference temperature: R ≈ R₀[1 + αΔT].
PowerP = VI = I²R = V²/RDistinguish heat in a resistor from total battery power.
Cell with internal resistanceV_terminal = ε − Ir (discharging)I = ε/(R+r) for one external resistance R.
Wheatstone balanceP/Q = R/SAt balance the galvanometer current is zero.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 97–135. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

4. Moving Charges & Magnetism

Force on a chargeF = q(v × B); |F| = |q|vB sinθMagnetic force does no work on a charge.
Circular motionr = mv/(|q|B); T = 2πm/(|q|B)For v perpendicular to uniform B.
Straight wire and loopB_wire = μ₀I/(2πr); B_loop centre = μ₀NI/(2R)Ideal geometries; N turns in the loop.
Ampère and solenoid∮B·dl = μ₀I_enclosed; B_solenoid ≈ μ₀nIn is turns per unit length; ideal long solenoid.
Current loopm = NIA; τ = mB sinθm is magnetic dipole moment.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 136–176. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

5. Magnetism & Matter

Magnetic dipole torqueτ = mB sinθ; U = −m·Bm denotes magnetic dipole moment.
Gauss’s law for magnetism∮B·dA = 0Net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero.
Material responseM = χ_m H; B = μ₀(H + M)For linear isotropic material: B = μ₀(1+χ_m)H.
Earth field componentsB_H = B cosδ; B_V = B sinδδ is dip angle, with components in the magnetic meridian.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 177–207. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

6. Electromagnetic Induction

FluxΦ_B = BA cosθAngle θ is between B and the area normal.
Faraday–Lenzε = −N dΦ_B/dtMagnitude for a uniform change: |ε| = N|ΔΦ|/Δt.
Motional emfε = BℓvStraight rod ℓ moving perpendicular to uniform B.
Self-inductionε_L = −L dI/dt; U_L = ½LI²Inductor opposes changes in current.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 208–236. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

7. Alternating Current

RMS valuesV_rms = V₀/√2; I_rms = I₀/√2Valid for sinusoidal AC.
ReactancesX_L = ωL; X_C = 1/(ωC); ω = 2πfPure L: current lags voltage; pure C: current leads.
Series LCRZ = √[R²+(X_L−X_C)²]; I_rms = V_rms/Ztanφ = (X_L−X_C)/R.
Resonanceω₀ = 1/√(LC); f₀ = 1/(2π√LC)At resonance: X_L=X_C, Z=R.
Power and transformerP_avg = V_rms I_rms cosφ; V_s/V_p = N_s/N_pIdeal transformer only; real losses exist.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 237–272. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.

8. Electromagnetic Waves

Vacuum speedc = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/sIn vacuum, c = fλ.
Field amplitudesE₀/B₀ = cFields are in phase for a plane wave in vacuum.
Displacement currentI_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dtImportant between charging capacitor plates.
IntensityI_avg = ½ ε₀c E₀²For a sinusoidal plane wave in vacuum.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, PDF pages 273–291. These are teaching notes, not quotations from the book.
